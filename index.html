SPOTS WE LOVE IN NYC

Food spots

Restaurants

  1. 01 Spicy Moon · Vegan Sichuan · Bowery

    The best vegan food.

  2. 02 Indienne NYC · Modern Indian · Hudson Yards

    Indian food presented as art pieces. A very unique experience ($$$$).

  3. 03 Kanyakumari · South Indian · Flatiron

    The best vada pav and thayir sadam bonda (Rahul’s fav).

  4. 04 HanGawi · Vegan Korean · Koreatown

    Vegan Korean food - and we live right across!

  5. 05 The Original Buddha Bodai · Vegan Chinese · Chinatown

    Vegan kosher Chinese food. You won’t miss your meat fix, we promise! Go to the one on Mott Street.

  6. 06 Chama Mama · Georgian · Chelsea

    Vijeta and Karan celebrated Rahul’s birthday here :) Branches in Manhattan and Brooklyn.

  7. 07 Shuka · Mediterranean · SoHo

    The best Mediterranean dips.

  8. 08 Veselka · Ukrainian · East Village

    Ukrainian food we love. Definitely get the pierogies! A surprisingly large number of veggie options. Rahul introduced Devika to this restaurant :) Also in Brooklyn.

  9. 09 Chinese Tuxedo · Chinese · Chinatown

    The best Chinese food in Chinatown. Ask them for a vegetarian menu.

  10. 10 Lucky Rabbit Noodles · Southeast Asian · DUMBO, Brooklyn

    A hole-in-the-wall spot for when you crave spicy Asian food.

  11. 11 Puglia · Italian · Little Italy

    In Little Italy, with many cute Italian restaurants around it.

  12. 12 Ippudo · Japanese ramen · East Village

    Our regular spot with Srividya and Jagan. DUMBO’s ippudo V has vegan options.

  13. 13 Sappe · Thai · Chelsea

    The Thai restaurant where we celebrated Rahul’s birthday.

  14. 14 Lungi · Sri Lankan · Upper East Side

    Sri Lankan food with the best jackfruit curry.

  15. 15 Awash · Ethiopian · East Village

    A very good Ethiopian restaurant.

  16. 16 Jungle Bird · Southeast Asian · Chelsea

    Aka how to make broccoli taste really good.

  17. 17 Old Fashion Cafe · Italian · SoHo

    Devika’s favorite, where she’s taken all her SD friends when they visit :)

  18. 18 Fellini Coffee · Italian cafe · SoHo

    A cute coffee shop with the best pistachio croissant.

Restaurants, continued

  1. 19 Ichiran · Japanese ramen · Times Square

    A booth-style ramen spot.

  2. 20 Dirt Candy · Vegetarian · Lower East Side

    A great veggie restaurant + great cocktails.

  3. 21 Thai Diner · Thai · Nolita

    A diner serving classic Thai food and great cocktails.

Just NY stuff

  • Fermento Pizza NYC · Italian pizza · West Village

    Sourdough crust, anyone?

  • L’Industrie · Italian pizza · Williamsburg, Brooklyn

    Check out the one in Williamsburg.

  • Crazy Pizza · Italian pizza · SoHo

    Recently discovered with Urvashi.

  • Apollo Bagels · Bagels · West Village

    The BEST bagel in town :)

  • Golden Diner · Asian diner · Two Bridges

    Eggy, greasy, delicious diner food. Try the honey butter pancakes and Thai tea tres leches!

Drinks spots

Cocktail bars, breweries, listening rooms & jazz

Cocktail bars

  1. 01 Double Chicken Please · Lower East Side

    Devika tried this and LOVES it. Arrive around 4:45 p.m. for a spot before the 5 p.m. opening.

  2. 02 Bangia · NoMad

    A Korean dive bar the whole NYC crew has been to. Prateek, Ashit and Ani introduced Devika to this spot.

  3. 03 Apotheke NoMad · NoMad

    The prettiest rooftop Vijeta and Devika discovered.

  4. 04 Apotheke Chinatown · Chinatown

    Where we celebrated Vijeta’s birthday and Ankit + Balya showed us their secret dance moves.

  5. 05 Patent Pending · NoMad

    A speakeasy Rahul took Devika to after Mars, Devika’s foster dog, chased him around the house (and bit him).

  6. 06 Saaqi · Tribeca

    The best Indian cocktails.

  7. 07 Death & Co · East Village

    Known for their cocktails.

  8. 08 Bar Pisellino · West Village

    A cute people-watching spot.

  9. 09 Lillie’s Victorian Establishment · Flatiron

    A beautifully decked-out cocktail bar.

  10. 10 The Campbell · Midtown East

    A historic bar inside Grand Central Terminal.

  11. 11 Bo Peep · Garment District

    One of the best music spots - you’re in for a surprise ;)

  12. 12 Sunken Harbor Club · Downtown Brooklyn

    A nautical-themed group favorite.

Breweries

  • KCBC · Bushwick, Brooklyn

    Kings County Brewers Collective. Great flights.

  • Threes Brewing · Greenpoint, Brooklyn

    Great food and beer. Also in Gowanus.

  • Finback · Gowanus, Brooklyn

    They specialize in hazy beers. Many branches, but Brooklyn is our favorite.
